Pizza

Pizza is delicious!

Pizza is a great dish, orginally from Italy. It has been made better here in America.

Ingredients

  • Dough
  • Tomato Sauce
  • Mozzarella
  • Pepperoni

Steps

  1. Kneed and place dough
  2. Spread tamato sauce on dough
  3. Add mozzzarella cheese
  4. Bake in oven at 400 degrees
  5. Take out and enjoy!
